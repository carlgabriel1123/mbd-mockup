What is Mind Body Designs?

Mind Body Designs is a US online shop for graphic T-shirts with positive, faith and fitness messages. Every design belongs to one of three collections: Mind (kindness and encouragement), Body (gym, running and yoga) and Spirit (Christian faith, family and friendship).

How much do the shirts cost?

Every tee is $34.95 in sizes S to XL. 2XL is $35.95, 3XL is $37.95 and 4XL is $38.95. Orders over $75 ship free in the United States, so any three shirts ship free.

Where are you located, and where are the shirts printed?

We are a US based company, and every order is printed in the USA.

How long does shipping take?

Each shirt is printed after you order it. Orders usually ship within 7 to 14 days, then take 2 to 5 business days to arrive anywhere in the US. You get a tracking email as soon as it ships.

Do you ship outside the United States?

Not yet. Right now we ship within the United States only.

What are the shirts made of, and how do they fit?

They are heavyweight, garment-dyed cotton tees with a relaxed unisex fit and a soft, broken-in feel. Sizes run S to 4XL. If you like an oversized look, size up.

What if my order arrives wrong or damaged?

Email us within 30 days and we will make it right with a reprint or a refund.
