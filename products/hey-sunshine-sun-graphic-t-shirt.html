About this design

Bring a little more light to ordinary days with this relaxed-fit T-shirt featuring a warm, retro-inspired “Hey Sunshine” design and a smiling sunburst. It feels like a bright morning, an open window, and the first good song of the day, an easy favorite for sunny-day strolls, weekend coffee runs, road trips, and anyone who carries a little optimism wherever they go. Made from heavyweight garment-dyed cotton, it has a soft, lived-in texture with lasting structure. Double-needle stitching and a seamless tubular knit add durability, while the broad size range makes it easy to find a comfortable fit. Wear it when the forecast is bright, or when you’re making your own sunshine. Product features - 100% pre-shrunk ring-spun US cotton for lasting comfort - Heavyweight 6.1 oz/yd² fabric with a relaxed fit - Garment-dyed for a soft color and lived-in texture - Double-needle stitching and tubular construction without side seams for durability - Available in sizes S, 4XL and 62 colors; made in Honduras Care instructions - Machine wash: cold (max 30C or 90F) - Do not bleach - Tumble dry: low heat - Iron, steam or dry: low heat - Do not dryclean
