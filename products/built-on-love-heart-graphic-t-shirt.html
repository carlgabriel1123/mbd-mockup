About this design

A heartfelt garment-dyed T-shirt featuring a vintage-inspired emblem with a heart at its center and the words “BUILT ON LOVE.” Laurel branches frame the design, giving it the feel of a keepsake badge, warm, grounded, and quietly meaningful. It speaks to people who believe the strongest homes, friendships, families, and communities are shaped by care. Wear it for everyday errands, relaxed weekends, family gatherings, anniversaries, or moments when a little extra love belongs in the room. Made from substantial ring-spun cotton with a relaxed fit, soft worn-in color, and durable construction, it feels at home with jeans, denim jackets, and favorite lived-in layers.

Fabric and feel

Heavyweight garment-dyed cotton with a soft, broken-in feel from the first wear.

Comes in 12 colors: Black, Red, White, Bay, Blossom, Blue Jean, Burnt Orange, Chambray, Ivory, Orchid, Pepper, True Navy.

Size and fit

Relaxed unisex fit, sizes S to 4XL. For an oversized look, size up.
