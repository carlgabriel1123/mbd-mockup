About this design

Carry a quiet reminder of faith wherever the day leads. This relaxed-fit T-shirt features a bold sunburst graphic surrounding the message “With God, all things are possible,” bringing warmth, courage, and hope to everyday moments. It has the grounded feel of a favorite tee, substantial, soft, and easy to wear with jeans, joggers, or layered under a jacket. Whether worn to church, a Bible study, a Christian gathering, or during an ordinary day that calls for extra strength, its message speaks simply and sincerely. Made from heavyweight, garment-dyed cotton with durable double-needle stitching, it is designed to become a trusted part of your wardrobe. Available in sizes S, 4XL and a wide range of colors.

Fabric and feel

Heavyweight garment-dyed cotton with a soft, broken-in feel from the first wear.

Comes in 12 colors: Black, Red, White, Bay, Blossom, Blue Jean, Burnt Orange, Chambray, Ivory, Orchid, Pepper, True Navy.

Relaxed unisex fit, sizes S to 4XL. For an oversized look, size up.
