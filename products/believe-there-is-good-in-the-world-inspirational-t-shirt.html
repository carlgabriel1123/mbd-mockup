About this design

A quiet reminder to carry with you: “Believe There Is Good in the World.” This relaxed garment-dyed T-shirt pairs a softly weathered look with uplifting typography, creating an easy everyday layer for kind-hearted optimists, encouragement-givers, and anyone choosing hope in small, ordinary moments. Wear it on a morning walk, while running errands, or when gathering with friends and family, it brings a little warmth to the room without saying too much. Made from heavyweight ring-spun cotton, it has a substantial feel, soft texture, and relaxed shape designed for comfortable daily wear.

Fabric and feel

Heavyweight garment-dyed cotton with a soft, broken-in feel from the first wear.

Comes in 12 colors: Black, Red, White, Bay, Blossom, Blue Jean, Chambray, Ivory, Orchid, Pepper, True Navy, Bright Orange.

Size and fit

Relaxed unisex fit, sizes S to 4XL. For an oversized look, size up.
